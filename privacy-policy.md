### Español

La aplicación gratuita **"Santo Rosario sin Internet"**, desarrollada por PABLO ANTONIO MARTINEZ MORILLAS, está diseñada respetando al máximo la privacidad de sus usuarios.

### English

The free application **"Santo Rosario sin Internet"**, developed by PABLO ANTONIO MARTINEZ MORILLAS, is designed with the utmost respect for the privacy of its users.
* **Data Collection:** The application does not collect, store, record, or share any type of personal information, device data, location data, or user identifiers.
